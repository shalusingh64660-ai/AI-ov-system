An online voting system is a digital platform that allows organizations to conduct elections and polls over the internet. Used primarily for private elections within corporations, associations, and clubs, its use for high-stakes government elections is rare due to significant security concerns. Estonia is a notable exception, as the only country to use internet voting for national elections.
