How to Use the Online Voting System

  • Step 1: Log in using your registered Aadhar and password.
  • Step 2: Verify your identity using the secure OTP sent to your email or phone.
  • Step 3: Select your candidate and click “Submit Vote”.
  • Step 4: Confirm your submission on the final confirmation screen.
  • Step 5: You will receive a digital confirmation receipt.
